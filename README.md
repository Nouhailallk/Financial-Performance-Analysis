# Financial Performance Analysis & Forecasting Dashboard

## Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze corporate financial performance and explore hypothetical business scenarios.

The project focuses on revenue, discounts, cost of goods sold (COGS), profitability, and performance across different business segments, countries, and products.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze gross sales, discounts, net sales, COGS, and profit.
- Monitor financial performance over time.
- Compare sales and profitability across countries, segments, and products.
- Evaluate the contribution of manufacturing costs.
- Explore how changes in sales, discounts, and manufacturing costs affect financial results.

## Dashboard Features

- Financial KPI monitoring.
- Quarterly Sales & Profit Trend.
- Revenue Erosion Waterfall.
- Net Sales by Country.
- Net Sales by Segment.
- Profit by Product.
- Segment Profitability Analysis.
- Manufacturing Cost Analysis.

## What-If Scenario Analysis

Interactive parameters allow users to explore hypothetical changes in:

- Sales Growth
- Discount Change
- Manufacturing Cost Change

The dashboard calculates scenario-based Net Sales, COGS, Gross Profit, and Gross Profit Margin.

These scenarios are illustrative sensitivity analyses, not statistical forecasts.
